# Code of Conduct

Sunder is an open-source project. We expect participation to be professional, respectful, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of differing experience levels and viewpoints.
- Keep discussion technical, constructive, and relevant.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Prefer clear explanations over personal criticism.
- Respect maintainers' time and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, secrets, exploit details, or private artifacts in public channels.
- Repeatedly pushing changes after maintainers have declined them without new information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers privately.
